Kunci Jawaban & Pembahasan Lengkap

Mata Pelajaran: English Grade X

Instansi: SMK Cyber Media

Tahun Ajaran: 2025/2026

PART A: PILIHAN GANDA (1 - 40)
PART B: ESSAY (KUNCI & PENJELASAN)
Essay 1: Personal Recount Paragraph
Kunci Jawaban Model: Last weekend, I visited Kuta Beach with my brother. We went there in the afternoon by motorcycle (Orientation). First, we bought some fresh coconut water. Then, we sat on the sand. After that, we took some photos when the beautiful sunset arrived (Events). It was a highly memorable holiday and I felt very happy (Reorientation). Pembahasan: Jawaban harus mengandung 3 elemen: Orientation (pembuka), Events (urutan kejadian), dan Reorientation (perasaan/penutup).
Essay 2: Simple Past Tense Error Analysis
Kunci Jawaban Model: 1. 'buy' -> 'bought' (V2 irregular). 2. 'readed' -> 'read' (V2 tetap namun beda pelafalan). 3. 'did not liked' -> 'did not like' (did not + V1). 4. 'die' -> 'died' (V2 regular). Pembahasan: Kalimat lampau wajib menggunakan Verb 2 kecuali setelah kata 'did not' yang harus kembali ke Verb 1.
Essay 3: Will vs Be Going To
Kunci Jawaban Model: 1. Answer: is going to rain (Evidence: heavy black clouds). 2. Answer: will go (Spontaneous decision: decision made at that moment). Pembahasan: 'Be going to' digunakan untuk bukti nyata, 'Will' digunakan untuk keputusan spontan.
Essay 4: Giving Opinion Dialogue
Kunci Jawaban Model: Andi: "What do you think of using AI for school?" (Asking) Budi: "Personally, I think it's very helpful." (Giving) Andi: "I agree with you. It's fast." (Agreeing) Budi: "But don't be lazy." Pembahasan: Dialog harus mencakup ungkapan menanyakan, memberikan, dan menyetujui pendapat.
Essay 5: Verb Transformation
Kunci Jawaban Model: 1. Sleep - Slept - Slept 2. Write - Wrote - Written 3. Bring - Brought - Brought 4. Cook - Cooked - Cooked Sentence: "I wrote a letter yesterday." Pembahasan: Past tense (V2) digunakan untuk aksi tunggal di masa lalu.
