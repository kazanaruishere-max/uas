PART A: Pilihan Ganda (Multiple Choice)

1. What is the main communicative purpose of the text above?
  • A. To describe a science museum's building in detail.
  • B. To persuade readers to study harder in physics.
  • C. To retell a past experience of visiting the science museum.
  • D. To explain the scientific steps of electricity generation.
Kunci Jawaban: C
Pembahasan: Teks tersebut merupakan Recount Text yang bertujuan menceritakan kembali pengalaman masa lalu.
2. Which generic structure of a recount text introduces 'who, when, and where' the story took place?
  • A. Events
  • B. Orientation
  • C. Reorientation
  • D. Resolution
Kunci Jawaban: B
Pembahasan: Orientation mengenalkan latar belakang cerita (tokoh, waktu, tempat).
3. 'First, we went to the space section...' What is the grammatical function of the underlined word 'First' in the recount text?
  • A. Specific participant
  • B. Action verb
  • C. Time connective
  • D. Adjective of quality
Kunci Jawaban: C
Pembahasan: 'First' berfungsi sebagai kata hubung waktu (time connective) untuk menunjukkan urutan kejadian.
4. What did the writer and classmates do right after trying the interactive physics experiments?
  • A. They watched a 3D movie about oceans.
  • B. They went to see the rocket replicas.
  • C. They had lunch at the cafeteria.
  • D. They took a bus journey home.
Kunci Jawaban: C
Pembahasan: Berdasarkan teks, setelah eksperimen fisika mereka makan siang di kafetaria.
5. Why did the writer use the past form words like 'went, tried, watched' instead of 'go, try, watch'?
  • A. To express a general habit.
  • B. Because the events happened in the past.
  • C. To describe future expectations.
  • D. Because it is an imperative/command sentence.
Kunci Jawaban: B
Pembahasan: Recount text menceritakan kejadian masa lampau, sehingga menggunakan Simple Past Tense.
6. Which of the following is a primary characteristic of a 'Personal Recount' text?
  • A. It utilizes first-person pronouns like 'I' or 'We'.
  • B. It tells a fictional story about talking animals.
  • C. It does not require any chronological order.
  • D. It discusses the writer's future plans.
Kunci Jawaban: A
Pembahasan: Personal recount memakai kata ganti orang pertama (I, we).
7. In a recount text structure, where can we usually find the writer's personal comments or feelings about the past event?
  • A. Orientation
  • B. Events
  • C. Reorientation
  • D. Introduction
Kunci Jawaban: C
Pembahasan: Reorientation berisi komentar atau perasaan penulis di akhir cerita.
8. A 'Historical Recount' is a type of recount text that aims to...
  • A. Tell the readers about imaginative dreams in space.
  • B. Give instructions on how to study history.
  • C. Retell a factual historical event in chronological order.
  • D. Offer opinions on historical movies.
Kunci Jawaban: C
Pembahasan: Historical recount menyajikan laporan kronologis sejarah nyata.
9. Our teacher, Mr. Tangguh, _______ English lessons at SMK Cyber Media every day.
  • A. teach
  • B. teaches
  • C. taught
  • D. teaching
Kunci Jawaban: B
Pembahasan: Subjek tunggal (Mr. Tangguh) dalam Simple Present Tense menggunakan V1 + -es.
10. The students _______ play football outside when it rains; they prefer staying inside.
  • A. does not
  • B. do not
  • C. are not
  • D. did not
Kunci Jawaban: B
Pembahasan: Subjek jamak (The students) menggunakan 'do not'.

PART B: Essay

Essay 1: Recount Text - Writing Experience

Write a simple, short Personal Recount paragraph (min. 5 sentences) about your activity during the last weekend.

Kunci Jawaban Referensi: Last weekend, I visited Kuta Beach with my brother. We went there in the afternoon by motorcycle (Orientation). First, we bought some fresh coconut water at a beach stall. Then, we sat on the sand and watched many tourists playing volleyball. After that, we took some photos when the beautiful sunset arrived (Events). It was a highly memorable holiday and I felt very happy after a stressful week at school (Reorientation).
Essay 2: Simple Past Tense - Error Analysis

Analyse the grammatical mistakes in the following past tense paragraph, and write down the corrected version: "Yesterday, my sister buy a new novel... She readed it..."

Kunci Jawaban Referensi: Analysis of Errors: 1. 'buy' -> 'bought' (V2 of buy is irregular). 2. 'readed' -> 'read' (pronounced /red/). 3. 'did not liked' -> 'did not like' (aux 'did not' + V1). 4. 'die' -> 'died' (regular V2). Corrected Paragraph: 'Yesterday, my sister bought a new novel at the bookstore. She read it happily in the living room. However, she did not like the ending because the main character died.'
Essay 3: Simple Future - Will vs Be Going To

1. "Look at those heavy black clouds! It _______ (rain) soon." 2. "We don't have any eggs." -> "Oh, really? I _______ (go) and buy a box."

Kunci Jawaban Referensi: 1. Answer: is going to rain Reason: Prediction based on visible physical evidence (black clouds). 2. Answer: will go Reason: Spontaneous decision made at the moment of speaking.
Essay 4: Giving Opinion - Dialogue Writing

Construct a brief dialogue between John and Jane discussing "Using AI in doing school assignments".

Kunci Jawaban Referensi: John: "Hey Jane, what do you think of using AI to do school assignments?" (Asking) Jane: "Personally, I think AI is extremely helpful." (Giving) John: "I agree with you. It helps me find bugs quickly." (Agreeing) Jane: "But, we must not rely on it too much." John: "I don't think so. It depends on how we use it." (Disagreeing)
Essay 5: Irregular & Regular Verbs

Transform: 1. Sleep, 2. Write, 3. Bring, 4. Cook. Construct one Past Tense sentence.

Kunci Jawaban Referensi: - Sleep -> Slept -> Slept - Write -> Wrote -> Written - Bring -> Brought -> Brought - Cook -> Cooked -> Cooked Example Sentence: "Yesterday, Kian wrote an impressive Python script for his coding assignment."
